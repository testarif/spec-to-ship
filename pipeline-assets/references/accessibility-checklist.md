# Accessibility checklist

- Use semantic HTML, a meaningful document title, and the correct page language.
- Ensure every control is keyboard-operable, has an accessible name, and exposes focus visibly.
- Preserve logical heading order and landmarks; use ARIA only when native semantics are insufficient.
- Provide sufficient text/control contrast and do not rely on color alone.
- Associate form labels and errors with their controls; announce important dynamic status changes appropriately.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid interactions that require precise pointer movement alone.
- Verify responsive layout and zoom do not hide content or controls.
